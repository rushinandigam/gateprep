How to use this plan

  • Each day is two or three blocks: a lecture block (90 min), a practice block (60 min on weekdays, 90 on weekends) and the DSA hour. Tick each block when it is done. Progress is saved in this browser.
  • Watch one primary channel per subject, then solve PYQs. A lecture is not complete until the PYQ set is done. PYQ source: gateoverflow.in.
  • General Aptitude takes 20 minutes of the practice block on Mon, Wed and Fri. Sundays carry a weekly sectional test and an error-log session.
  • Fall behind? Do not squeeze two days into one. Drop the practice block's last half and move on, then recover on Sunday.
  • This is a fast first pass: about 83 days to cover all 10 syllabus sections. If a subject is new to you, spend weekend extra hours there. Algorithms, OS, DBMS and CN carry the most repeat marks.
  • The official 2027 syllabus has no P/NP section, so NP-completeness is a skim only. The calendar ends on Jan 31. For Feb 1 to 7 use the last-week plan in your handbook (formulas and weak topics, one PYQ paper, quick revision, admit card, rest).
